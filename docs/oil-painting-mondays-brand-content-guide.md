# Oil Painting Mondays Brand and Content Guide

## Brand Core

### Working Name

Oil Painting Mondays

### Brand Type

Women’s art club / artist community

### Brand Positioning

A refined women’s painting club in Broomfield, Colorado, presenting original works, shared practice, and a sense of cultural intimacy.

## Brand Attributes

- Elegant
- Artistic
- Curated
- Warm
- Editorial
- Contemporary
- Trustworthy
- Quietly premium

## Tone of Voice

### Should sound like

- clear
- poised
- warm
- observant
- confident
- inviting

### Should not sound like

- salesy
- trendy in a tech way
- overly poetic
- childish
- generic nonprofit language
- mass-market retail

## Messaging Pillars

### Community

The club brings together women artists through shared creative practice.

### Craft

The work deserves close looking, thoughtful presentation, and careful language.

### Collection

Visitors can discover and reserve original works in a calm and transparent way.

## Homepage Messaging Priorities

1. Explain what the club is.
2. Show that the art is real and collectible.
3. Show the artists.
4. Show how buying works.
5. Make contact feel easy.

## Suggested Headline Directions

- Original works by a women’s painting community in Broomfield, Colorado.
- A women’s art club centered on practice, presence, and collected beauty.
- Paintings made in community, presented with care.
- Oil paintings to discover, collect, and live with.

## Suggested Supporting Copy Directions

- Oil Painting Mondays is a women’s artist community devoted to creative practice, connection, and the presentation of original work.
- The club brings together painters whose work reflects a range of techniques, styles, and personal voices.
- Visitors can explore available paintings online and submit a request to reserve a work for purchase.

## CTA Language

### Preferred

- View Gallery
- Meet the Artists
- Explore Events
- Reserve This Painting
- Inquire to Purchase
- Contact the Club

### Avoid

- Buy instantly
- Shop now
- Get started
- Unlock more
- Join the revolution

## Visual Brand Direction

### Image behavior

- large artwork crops
- quiet transitions
- no busy decorative frames
- minimal UI around images
- image-first gallery storytelling

### Layout behavior

- editorial rhythm
- asymmetry in selected sections
- strong margins
- not card-heavy everywhere
- calm filtering interface

## Color Direction

### Approved direction

- soft ivory
- charcoal
- deep plum
- muted slate
- cool gray

### Avoid

- brown
- orange
- red
- green

## Typography Direction

### Display style

Elegant serif with magazine-like presence.

### Body style

Clean sans-serif with excellent readability.

## Content Rules

- Keep paragraphs short.
- Avoid exaggerated emotional language.
- Be specific and grounded.
- Let the artwork carry visual drama.
- Use concise descriptions for paintings.
- Keep trust and process language clear.

## Painting Copy Template

### Short artwork description formula

Use:

- visual mood
- medium/approach
- one sentence of atmosphere or detail

Example structure:

"An atmospheric oil painting with layered brushwork and a quiet tonal palette."

## Artist Bio Template

Use 2–4 short sentences:

- who the artist is
- what themes or techniques they explore
- how they work or what distinguishes the work

## Trust Language for Purchase Flow

Use calm and transparent phrasing.

Examples:

- Submit a reservation request and the club will confirm availability.
- Payment is arranged after confirmation.
- Shipping and local pickup options are available.
- Availability may change until the request is confirmed.

## Editorial Design Summary

If a designer or AI builder uses this guide, the site should feel like a small gallery journal with a purchase pathway, not a store catalogue or nonprofit brochure.
